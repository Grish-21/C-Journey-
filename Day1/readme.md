Day 1: Sum and Reverse of Digits

Problem: Read a positive integer n from the user. Print:

The sum of its digits
The number with its digits reversed
Whether it is a palindrome (same as its reverse)

Example

Input:  12321
Sum of digits: 9
Reversed: 12321
Palindrome: yes

Input:  4506
Sum of digits: 15
Reversed: 6054
Palindrome: no

Rules:

Use only <iostream>. Do not convert to a string.
Use a while loop with % and /.
Put the logic in functions, e.g. int digitSum(int n) and int reverseNumber(int n).
